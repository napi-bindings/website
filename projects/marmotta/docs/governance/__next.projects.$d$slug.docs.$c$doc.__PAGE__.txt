<h2 id="lead-maintainers"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#lead-maintainers">Lead Maintainers</a></h2>
<p>Marmotta Lead Maintainers are the organization owners.
They are the only members of the <code>@napi-bindings/leads</code> team. The Lead
Maintainers are the curator of the Marmotta project and their key responsibility
is to issue releases of Marmotta and its dependencies.</p>
<h2 id="collaborators"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#collaborators">Collaborators</a></h2>
<p>Marmotta Collaborators maintain the projects of the napi-bindings organization.</p>
<p>Collaborators have:</p>
<ul>
<li>Commit access to the projects repository</li>
<li>Grant to release new versions of the project</li>
</ul>
<p>Both Collaborators and non-Collaborators may propose changes to the source code
of the projects of the organization. The mechanism to propose such a change is a
GitHub pull request. Collaborators review and merge (<em>land</em>) pull requests
following the <a href="/projects/marmotta/docs/contributing/#rules">CONTRIBUTING</a> guidelines.</p>
<h3 id="collaborator-activities"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#collaborator-activities">Collaborator activities</a></h3>
<ul>
<li>Helping users and novice contributors</li>
<li>Contributing code and documentation changes that improve the project</li>
<li>Reviewing and commenting on issues and pull requests</li>
<li>Participation in working groups</li>
<li>Merging pull requests</li>
<li>Release plugins</li>
</ul>
<p>After two years of inactivity, the Lead Maintainers can
remove inactive Collaborators or provide them with
<em>Past Collaborators</em> status.
Past Collaborators may request that the Lead
Maintainers restore them to active status.</p>
<h2 id="great-contributors"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#great-contributors">Great Contributors</a></h2>
<p>Great contributors on a specific area in the napi-bindings ecosystem will be invited
to join this group by Lead Maintainers. This group has the same permissions of a
contributor.</p>
<h2 id="collaborator-nominations"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#collaborator-nominations">Collaborator nominations</a></h2>
<p>Individuals making significant and valuable contributions to the project may be
a candidate to join the napi-bindings organization.</p>
<p>A Collaborator needs to open a private team discussion on GitHub and list the
candidates they want to sponsor with a link to the user's contributions. For
example:</p>
<ul>
<li>Activities in the napi-bindings organization
<code>[USERNAME](https://github.com/search?q=author:USERNAME+org:napi-bindings)</code></li>
</ul>
<p>Otherwise, a Contributor may self-apply if they believe they meet the above
criteria by reaching out to a Lead Maintainer privately with the links to their
valuable contributions. The Lead Maintainers will reply to the Contributor and
will decide whether the candidate should be made a Collaborator.</p>
<p>The consensus to grant a new candidate Collaborator status is reached when:</p>
<ul>
<li>at least one of the Lead Maintainers approve</li>
<li>at least two of the Collaborators approve</li>
</ul>
<p>After these conditions are satisfied, the <a href="/projects/marmotta/docs/contributing/#onboarding-collaborators">onboarding
process</a> may start.</p>
<h2 id="lead-maintainers-nominations"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#lead-maintainers-nominations">Lead Maintainers nominations</a></h2>
<p>A Team Member may be promoted to a Lead Maintainers only through nomination by a
Lead maintainer and with agreement from the rest of Lead Maintainers.</p>
