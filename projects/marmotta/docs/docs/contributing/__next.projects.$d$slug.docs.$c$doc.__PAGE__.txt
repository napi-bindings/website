19:T1114,<h1 id="contributing-to-marmotta">Contributing To Marmotta</h1>
<p><a id="contributing"></a></p>
<p>Thank you for taking an interest in contributing to Marmotta. We are excited to
receive your support and knowledge. This guide is our attempt to help you help
us.</p>
<blockquote>
<h2 id="note"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#note">Note</a></h2>
<p>This is an informal guide. For full details, please review the formal
<a href="/projects/marmotta/docs/contributing/">CONTRIBUTING
document</a>
and our <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Developer_Certificate_of_Origin" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Developer Certificate of
Origin</a>.</p>
</blockquote>

<h2 id="types-of-contributions-were-looking-for"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#types-of-contributions-were-looking-for">Types Of Contributions We're Looking For</a></h2>
<p><a id="contribution-types"></a></p>
<p>In short, we welcome any type of contribution you are willing to provide. No
contribution is too small. We gladly accept contributions such as:</p>
<ul>
<li>Documentation improvements: from small typo corrections to major document
reworks</li>
<li>Helping others by answering questions in pull requests and issues</li>
<li>Fixing <a href="https://github.com/napi-bindings/marmotta/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Abug" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">known
bugs</a></li>
<li>Reporting previously unknown bugs by opening an issue with a minimal
reproduction</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="ground-rules--expectations"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#ground-rules--expectations">Ground Rules &#x26; Expectations</a></h2>
<p><a id="contributing-rules"></a></p>
<p>Before we get started, here are a few things we expect from you (and that you
should expect from others):</p>
<ul>
<li>Be respectful and thoughtful in your conversations around this project. This
project is maintained by a diverse set of people from all across the globe.
Each person has their own views and opinions about the project. Try to listen
to each other and reach an agreement or compromise.</li>
<li>We have a <a href="https://github.com/napi-bindings/marmotta/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Code of
Conduct</a>. You
must adhere to it to participate in this project.</li>
<li>If you open a pull request, please ensure your contribution passes all
tests. If there are test failures, you will need to address them before we can
merge your contribution.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="how-to-contribute"><a class="heading-anchor" href="#how-to-contribute">How To Contribute</a></h2>
<p><a id="contributing-how-to"></a></p>
<p>If you'd like to contribute, start by searching through the
<a href="https://github.com/napi-bindings/marmotta/issues" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">issues</a> and <a href="https://github.com/napi-bindings/marmotta/pulls" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">pull
requests</a> to see whether someone else
has raised a similar idea or question.</p>
<p>If you don't see your idea listed, and you think it fits into the goals of this
guide, do one of the following:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>If your contribution is minor,</strong> such as a typo fix, open a pull request.</li>
<li><strong>If your contribution is major,</strong> such as a new feature, start by opening an
issue first. That way, other people can weigh in on the discussion before you
do any work.</li>
</ul>
